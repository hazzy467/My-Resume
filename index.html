UNITY 2D • SURVIVAL & PLATFORMER

Dark Forest (Inspired by Backcountry)

A 2D survival game inspired by true wilderness survival events. Players must navigate through a perilous forest across three distinct levels, managing health, dodging dangerous wildlife, and escaping predators to survive.

  • Level 1 (Frostbite Trail): Thermal management mechanics — navigate treacherous cold paths while maintaining body temperature near campfire heat sources to prevent rapid health depletion.
  • Level 2 (The Cavern): Cave exploration & hazard avoidance — dodge snakes and bats while collecting wild fruits to restore health.
  • Level 3 (Apex Chase): High-stakes chase mechanics — escape the wilderness while being pursued by a bear while avoiding environmental obstacles.

UNITY 2D • EDUCATIONAL & ENVIRONMENT

Earth Mission

An interactive educational 2D game designed to raise environmental awareness through fun cleanup mechanics, waste sorting systems, and educational quizzes across three engaging levels.

  • Level 1 (Park Clean-Up): Collect litter scattered around the park and deposit it into the trash bin to restore cleanliness.
  • Level 2 (Waste Sorting): Categorize collected waste into correct recycling bins (Plastic, Glass, and Paper) to teach recycling principles.
  • Level 3 (Eco-Quiz Challenge): Answer environmental awareness quizzes to test knowledge and complete the mission.
